INSIDE EXODUS STUDIO

Exodus Studio Productions is a premier audio production and post-production facility dedicated to delivering high-quality, customized sound solutions. We specialize in recording, producing, and composing original music for songwriters, bands, TV, film, multimedia projects, voice-overs, and radio.

Cutting-Edge Technology & Expertise

At Exodus Studio, we leverage the latest in digital hard disk recording equipment and advanced software to ensure top-tier results for every project. Our software suite includes industry-leading tools such as:

  • Pulsar (Creamware)
  • Cubase & Wavelab (Steinberg)
  • Acid (Sonic Foundry)
  • Reason, Atmosphere, Edirol, Emagic, Kompakt, Native Instruments

In addition, our diverse selection of keyboard-based equipment from trusted brands like Roland, Korg, Yamaha, and Alesis ensures versatility and precision in sound creation. Our outboard gear includes high-quality equipment from Alesis Adat, Mackie, Focusrite, ART, and Audio Technica, all designed to enhance the recording and mixing process.

Comprehensive Services for Every Need

We offer a full range of services to suit every stage of your audio production, including:

  • Recording & Music Production: Whether you're an artist looking to record your demo, a band working on an album, or a company needing custom music for a multimedia project, we bring your vision to life with professionalism and precision.
  • Remixing & Editing: We specialize in the remixing and editing of existing tracks, ensuring your music sounds polished and ready for release, whether it’s for radio, film, or digital platforms.
  • Live Streaming Services: Our studio is fully equipped for professional live streaming via Zoom, providing a seamless experience for virtual events, performances, and broadcasts.

A Team of Experts at Your Service

At Exodus Studio, we’re proud to have a team of professional, skilled musicians and audio experts who bring invaluable insight to your project. Our staff’s experience spans various aspects of the music industry, and they’re here to offer support and guidance every step of the way.

A Relaxing, Creative Environment

We understand that the creative process requires a space that fosters inspiration and focus. Our studio is designed to provide a comfortable, relaxing environment where you can bring your ideas to life—from the initial concept to the finished product.
