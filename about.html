Founder

Anthony Paiano

Anthony Paiano, a musician, composer, producer, and engineer, was born in Toronto, Ontario, Canada. His musical journey began at a young age, taking full advantage of the diverse music programs offered in his school years. This passion for music led him to pursue a formal education in jazz piano at Humber College in Toronto, where he honed his skills and deepened his love for music.

As his professional career took off, Anthony performed as a pianist and keyboardist, both locally and internationally, gaining experience and exposure across the globe. His passion for music, however, soon led him to a new endeavor—founding Exodus Studio Productions. Here, he combined his skills as a producer, composer, and engineer to support local artists and corporate clients in the Greater Toronto Area.

Anthony believes that to succeed in today’s competitive music industry, a studio owner must master both the art of music composition and the technical side of production. "Being a producer, composer, and engineer are essential components of modern music production," says Anthony. "The creative side of composing is just as important as the technical precision needed to meet industry standards."

With decades of experience in both the music and production fields, Anthony continues to innovate, bringing fresh, high-quality content to the world of music and multimedia.
