THE PROCESS

HOW IT WORKS

  1. Send your song/demo

    Share your song, demo or idea, and tell me about your goals.

  2. Discuss your vision

    We'll talk about your style, ideas and what you want to achieve.

  3. I produce

    I arrange, record, edit and produce your song using professional studio techniques.

  4. You review & give feedback

    You'll listen to the work, share your feedback, and we'll make adjustments together.

  5. Final production delivered

    Once everything is right, you'll receive the completed track(s) ready for release.

WHAT I OFFER

SERVICES

Professional services to help you create, develop and finish your music.

  • Full music production

    From idea to finished track. I'll help you create a polished, professional production.

  • Arrangement

  • Remote vocal recording

  • Original composition
